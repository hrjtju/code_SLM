### 相关问题

![600](Pasted%20image%2020240624230647.png)

* **Operational**
    * *Part decomposition*
        * larger part that in its current form exceeds the dimensions of the build space into multiple smaller parts
        * printability problem
        * Part decomposition is also used to maximize productivity (production time, cost, and part quality); mechanical functionality; artistry; interchangeability; and to avoid the need for support structures
        * ==Chopper==. In their study, the focus was only on part decomposition, with no consideration of nesting concerns
        * ==Dapper==. decompose an object into minor parts which can be efficiently packed. aims to minimize support material, build time, and the assembly cost
        * ==partitioning-and-packing==. partitioning component is based on a level set representation
        * ==divide-and-conquer==. derived through a near-convex decomposition
        * Refer to 
            * Oh, Y., Zhou, C., Behdad, S., 2018. Part decomposition and assembly-based (Re) design for additive manufacturing: a review. Addit. Manuf. 22, 230–242. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.addma.2018.04.018.
    * *Part acceptance*
      schedulingproblem and occurs whenever the demand exceeds the available capacity of the resources, requiring the rejection of some orders
      “if it can be produced within one of the machinesand completed within the delivery time requested by the customer” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 6](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=6&annotation=3Z3LE38V))
        * **1: the set of orders from different customers is known a priori**
        * **2: not all orders are known at the beginning of the decision period**
            * “Tafakkori et al. (2022) suggested a framework that considers negotiating with customers to change their desired due dates and assess the influence on the order acceptance and scheduling.” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 6](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=6&annotation=CNSD84IA))
            * *Meta-heuristics ?*
                * distribution
                * future information
                * 来了再说

    * *Part distribution*

### 2 主路径分析

![600](Pasted%20image%2020240624233146.png)

* **开创性工作（Seminal Studies）**：
    * ==Ikonen et al. (1997)== ：**Genetic Algorithm** to solve a 3D nesting problem
        * “in which parts are represented as non-convex geometries, *including holes and cavities where other smaller parts can be placed*” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 7](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=7&annotation=4Q7YEQUK))
        * “place large parts first and fill the remainingspace with smaller ones” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 7](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=7&annotation=WP8GUGLN))
        * “A second decision was the orientation of each part in the SLS machine. Each part had 24 allowed orientations (45 degrees of increment around each coordinate axis)” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 7](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=7&annotation=5PF4B8HQ))
    * ==Li et al. (2017)== ：**MILP model** for planning and scheduling in AM
        * considered an objective function based on the *production cost* per unit volume of material on non-identical SLM machines
        * “The optimum allocation of parts into such AM machines was solved by a MIP model,and two heuristic procedures best fit and adapted best fit – are developed” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 7](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=7&annotation=P8CEG8U4))
    * ==Chergui et al. (2018)== ：Integrate planning and scheduling using **a heuristic based on the EDD rule**
        * “a series of order due dates were considered and allocated to identical parallel machines” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 7](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=7&annotation=YG8AED76))
        * “the time spent on the AM machines is expressed in terms of part’s total production area and material volume” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 7](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=7&annotation=QSWEDW4Q))
        * “minimizethe tardiness and maximize the utilization of the machines” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 7](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=7&annotation=M9BYUL4B))
* **分箱问题（Nesting Problem）**
    * ==Ikonen et al. (1997), Hur et al. (2001)==：modified Bottom-Left approach + GA
        * “the orientation of the parts is optimized by considering their heights to achieve the minimum build height, reducing the production time and improving the surface quality” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 7](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=7&annotation=HHZV7B4E))
        * “adopting the minimum build height as the objective function for problems involving dependent parts, resulting from the division of a larger part (part decomposition problem), is the most fitting choice, given the rough surfaces that emerge from the part decomposition process.” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 7](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=7&annotation=KU58ICF4))
    * ==Canellidis et al. (2006)== ： bbox + LD
        * “The use of support structures increases the build time, as this includes the time needed to actually produce the parts and the time required for support removal, and surface finishing” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 7](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=7&annotation=HCR5GANE))
        * minimum bbox
        * “Left-Border-Down-Border rule was applied by a GeneticAlgorithm for a serialplacement solution” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 8](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=8&annotation=PBW58N4T))
    * ==Canellidis et al. (2013)== ： Polygon Bounding + No-Fit-Polygon
        * approxi mating the parts geometry through polygons instead of the common bounding box
        * No-Fit-Polygon is employed as an additional placing rule based on the former Left-Border-Down-Border
    * ==Gogate and Pande (2008)== ：3D Nesting Problem optimising part orientation
        * By assigning different weights to interpolate between the different evaluation criteria, the problem can be custom ized to meet the functional requirements of several AM machines
        * *Bottom-Left method* is implemented by a Genetic Algorithm
        * Parts are represented through voxel models and could be rotated around the build axis with an increment of 45 degrees
    * ==Zhang et al. (2016)== ：Solving 2D nesting by 2-stage Algorithm using GA
        * “first a AM feature-based orientation involves determining the optimal build orientation for each part to ensure high production quality” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 8](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=8&annotation=6VJKN4UI))
        * “second a parallel optimization model is used to maximize the compactness of the nesting problem through the use of polygons to represent the geometry of the parts” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 8](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=8&annotation=7LMKRBMI))
* **规划问题（Scheduling Problem）**
    * ==Ransikarbum et al. (2017)== ：MILP for non-identical parallel FDM
    * ==Fera et al. (2018)== ：GA for single machine scheduling
    * ==Kucukkoc (2019)== ：3 MILP models for different schemes (single, parallel, non-identical parallel)
        * “results showed *limited applicability when problem size increases*, which indicates the need for effective heuristics and metaheuristics to solve large problem instances” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 8](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=8&annotation=8CS8KZ5U))
    * ==Oh et al. (2020)== ：Review in nesting and scheduling problems in AM
    * ==Fera et al. (2020)== ：tabu search
    * ==Yılmaz (2020)== ：two-stage
      parts are produced on SLM machines by one manufacturer in the production step and then distributed to various customers by means of solving a transportation problem
* **分箱规划一体化问题（Integrated Nesting-Scheduling Problem）**
    * ==Alicastro et al. (2021)== ：Q-Learning variable neighborhood search
    * ==Rohaninejad et al. (2022)== ：non-identical SLM + bi-objective MILP (makespan + *total tardiness cost*) + NGSA-II
        * “parts belonging to a particular job must be of the same material type” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 8](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=8&annotation=KNBKN56P))
        * “while different material types can be used for successive builds” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 8](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=8&annotation=KLUHLCHC))
        * “This setup process requires additional time, and the setuptime is sequence-dependent” ([Pinto et al., 2024, p. 9](zotero://select/library/items/FJ3R63QP)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9R2Z9362?page=9&annotation=YN58EHV7))
    * ==Tafakkori et al. (2022)== ：multi-objective model on *profit*, *energy utilization*, and *goodwill losses* + part orientation + irregular geometries ... + robust improved ε-constraint method, NSGA-II, multi-objective metaheuristic Grey Wolf Optimizer

### 其他讨论

**Nesting**
* **主要矛盾在于构建和求解优化问题的困难性**
* 对于零件几何性质的考量，大部分研究者采用最小矩形、圆柱或球形对其进行建模
* 目标函数：最高零件尺寸、其他启发性目标函数（如零件间距 etc.）
* 决策方式：串行/并行  
* 工件朝向的确定：两步法，先确定朝向，再确定位置（更容易计算）；一步法（困难）

**Scheduling**
* 最小化makespan、tardiness
* upstream和downstream操作较少被考虑
* 涉及多种材料的Scheduling --> *Family-wise Job Scheduling*
* 云制造环境、混合制造
* 对于多条件的研究依然有限（如Flow Shop和Job Shop同时出现）
* Benchmark ?

**常用算法**
* 元启发：GA、SA
* 基于规则：如左下
* 数学规划：MIP、MILP
* 强化学习（少）(没有纯粹的RL，静态问题、动态问题)

### 未来的工作

1. 更加接近真实情况
    1. 使建模中的零件几何构型更加接近现实
    2. 考虑真实情况中的概率事件（如需求不确定性、机器参数的不确定性、机器可用性的不确定性）
2. 有关解决相关问题
    1. 对下游任务的研究较少（distribution）
    2. 在装箱中考虑零件解构的可能（part decomposition）
    3. 考虑库存整合问题（inventory issues），如供应链管理、多余零件管理（和普通制造不同）
3. 结合实际的AM场景
    1. 考虑结合云制造、多机器人协同等方面
    2. 考虑多材料增材制造、多激光、多喷头、多轴增材制造装备和多工步/工序制造
4. 将AM与现有工艺相结合
    1. 在考虑范围中增加传统制造装备供选择（AM or 传统制造）
    2. 考虑增材制造件的后处理、返工和修复操作
5. 考虑新的求解方法
    1. 元启发算法 + 机器学习
    2. 通过类3D视觉模型分析实体模型，以得出最佳朝向
6. 环境可持续
    1. **最小化耗能**
    2. 追踪废物排放量
    3. 优化原料用量
    4. 降低材料浪费和返工/重造概率
